Privacy Policy

Canya is a desktop app that works without an account. This page explains what happens to your data when you use the app, this website, and the optional Canya Teams service.

Last updated: 14 October 2026 · Türkçe: KVKK Aydınlatma Metni

Who is responsible

Canya is made and run by Burak Kaya, an individual based in Türkiye, who is the data controller for the processing described here. Questions and requests: dev@burkaya.com.

The desktop app

Your projects are files on your own computer. The app has no telemetry, analytics or crash reporting, and it does not send your diagrams anywhere. Its error log stays on your device. The app only goes online in these cases:

  • Update check — at start-up the app fetches the latest release information from GitHub, where Canya's releases are hosted. GitHub sees your IP address. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Import from a link — only when you paste a link and ask for it, the app downloads that one address.
  • Feedback — the feedback buttons open a form or a GitHub issue in your browser; the app itself sends nothing.
  • Canya Teams — only after you sign in, as described below.

This website

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the pages, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Page views and download clicks are counted with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and builds no profile of you.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google, which sees your IP address.
  • Your light/dark theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage and never leaves it.

Canya Teams: what we store

Canya Teams (share links, project sync and live collaboration) is optional and needs an account. When you use it, we store:

  • Your account — the sign-in provider you chose (GitHub or Google), that provider's user ID for you, your e-mail address, display name and profile picture address.
  • Sign-in sessions — a hashed session token, the device's user agent and when it was last used. Sessions last 30 days and are renewed while you keep using the app; the app keeps its token in your operating system's keychain.
  • Subscription — trial dates, subscription status and the customer and subscription IDs from our payment provider. We never see your card details.
  • Your content, encrypted — shared links, synced projects and shared projects are encrypted on your device before upload, and the keys stay with you (a share link's key sits after the #, which browsers never send to a server). We cannot read your diagrams or their titles. We do see sizes, dates, link expiry, who belongs to which shared project and with what role, and invitation records.
  • Live collaboration — edits and cursors are relayed encrypted between participants and are not stored on their own; the shared project itself is stored encrypted like a synced project.
  • Technical data — your IP address is used in memory to limit abusive request rates and is not written to our logs. Request logs record the method, path, status and duration.

The only cookie is a short-lived, signed one in your browser during sign-in, which ties the provider's answer to your sign-in attempt. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service you signed up for — performance of a contract.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse — our legitimate interest.
  • To keep subscription and accounting records the law requires — legal obligation.

We do not sell your data, show ads or use your data for profiling.

Who else is involved

  • GitHub and Google — when you sign in with them they share your account ID, e-mail address, name and picture with us, under their own privacy policies.
  • Lemon Squeezy — sells Canya Teams subscriptions as merchant of record. It collects your payment details and billing address as an independent controller under its privacy policy and tells us only the subscription status and IDs.
  • Hosting — the Canya Teams servers and backups run in Türkiye.

GitHub, Google and Lemon Squeezy operate outside Türkiye, mainly in the United States, so using them involves transferring data abroad.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: for as long as your account exists.
  • Expired share links: removed 30 days after they expire.
  • After a subscription ends: links stop working after a 30-day grace period, and the encrypted content of links and synced projects is deleted 90 days after the subscription ended.
  • Backups: kept for 14 days, then overwritten. Your content in them is encrypted like everywhere else.

You can unpublish links and stop syncing yourself at any time; that deletes the stored copies. To delete your whole account, write to dev@burkaya.com from the address on the account.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to its use, or ask what has been done with it. Write to dev@burkaya.com; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to a data protection authority — in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK), in the EU the authority of your country.

Canya Teams is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

When this policy changes we update this page and the date above. Significant changes are announced in the app or by e-mail before they apply.
